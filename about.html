About MF Dashboard

Our mission is to democratise mutual fund investing by providing free, data-driven insights to every Indian investor.

Our Mission

India Mutual Fund Dashboard was built with one goal: to make professional-grade mutual fund research accessible to every Indian investor — whether they are investing their first ₹500 or managing a multi-crore portfolio.

We believe that informed investing leads to better outcomes. Too many retail investors make decisions based on hearsay or recent performance. We aim to change that by presenting comprehensive, easy-to-understand data on all major Indian mutual fund schemes.

Our platform is completely free, not SEBI registered, and maintained as an educational resource. Always consult a qualified financial advisor before making investment decisions.

⚠️ Important: Please read before investing.

The information provided on India Mutual Fund Dashboard is for educational and informational purposes only. It does not constitute investment advice, financial advice, trading advice, or any other sort of advice.

We are not registered with SEBI as an investment advisor or research analyst. The fund data displayed is sourced from publicly available information and may not be real-time or complete. Always verify data from official sources like AMFI and the fund house's own website before investing.

Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks. Past performance is not indicative of future results. The NAV of funds can go up or down depending on the forces of supply and demand in the securities market.

Before investing, read all scheme-related documents carefully. Consider consulting a SEBI-registered investment advisor who understands your specific financial situation and goals.
