Debt vs Hybrid — Quick Comparison

🏦 Debt Funds
  • ✅ Lower risk than equity
  • ✅ Stable, predictable returns (6–9%)
  • ✅ Suitable for 1–3 year goals
  • ✅ Better than FD post-tax for higher brackets
  • ⚠️ Interest rate risk for long-duration funds
  • ⚠️ Returns not guaranteed
⚖️ Hybrid Funds
  • ✅ Auto-rebalancing between equity & debt
  • ✅ Moderate risk, moderate returns (12–18%)
  • ✅ Suitable for 3–5 year goals
  • ✅ Single fund diversification
  • ⚠️ Equity portion carries market risk
  • ⚠️ Expense ratios can be higher
